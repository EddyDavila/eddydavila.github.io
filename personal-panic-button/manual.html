YOUR GUIDE TO PERSONAL PANIC BUTTON

Stay ready.
Keep trusted people close.

Set up your contacts before you need help. When you activate an alert, PPB can share your available location and help you reach the people you trust.

User manual · Updated November 14, 2026 · App reference 1.0.21 (22)

PPB is a user-activated safety tool, not a monitored emergency-response service. If you need urgent assistance, use an available direct contact method and the appropriate local emergency number.

1. Set up before you need help

  1. Create your PPB account. Choose Create account or Sign in when the app opens. Use your PPB app password, not your Google password. For a new account, open the verification email, follow its link, return to PPB and check verification.
  2. Read the safety notice. Review it before continuing. For help later, open More > Help & how to use.
  3. Add someone you trust. In Contacts, add a consenting person's name, phone number and optional email. Free access selects one active contact; a verified subscription allows additional contacts.
  4. Choose location sharing. Read the separate location disclosure and grant the Android permissions you agree to. Location sharing is optional: you can still prepare a help message without it.
  5. Check readiness. Review More > App account and Emergency reliability. Your verified PPB account normally connects the phone automatically. Review the local emergency number in More so it is appropriate for your location.

Connect Gmail for automatic email

Open More > Automatic Gmail alerts > Connect Gmail. Choose your own Google account and approve the app's send-only permission. This is separate from signing into PPB and does not give the app access to read your inbox.

Use Send test to myself and check that mailbox for the clearly marked test. The self-test contains no emergency location and does not notify your trusted contacts. If Google does not allow the connection, automatic Gmail is not ready; use another available contact method.

Add the home-screen widget

On Samsung, long-press an empty area of the home screen, tap Widgets, find Personal Panic Button and add it. The widget opens PPB's normal activation flow; it does not bypass account, contact, location or demo checks.

2. Start an alert

  1. Tap the panic control in PPB or tap its home-screen widget. Complete any setup or demo notice shown.
  2. A five-second cancellation countdown begins. Tap Cancel if it was accidental, or Activate now to skip the remaining countdown.
  3. PPB records the alert. Connected Gmail can attempt an automatic email to eligible contact addresses. With location enabled and tracking access available, the phone starts collecting and sharing available location points.
  4. Review the SMS draft and tap Send in your messaging app. SMS is not sent automatically.
  5. To call, use the active alert's Call control. It opens your dialer with the configured number; you place the call.

The five seconds is a cancellation window, not a delivery guarantee. PPB does not automatically call emergency services, detect a fall or dispatch help. The status on your phone tells you whether email is queued, accepted, failed or uncertain; accepted does not mean delivered or read.

Optional minute email reminders

Reminders are off until you separately enable them for future alerts. Tell contacts what to expect. While tracking is active, eligible follow-ups are attempted no more often than once a minute, with at most 60 follow-ups per alert. All app mail is capped at 100 submission attempts and 400 recipient deliveries per rolling 24 hours; Gmail may impose stricter limits.

End, tracking expiry, disconnection, disabling reminders or an unresolved sending failure stops further reminders. Missed reminders are not sent in a burst after reconnection.

End an alert

Tap End alert to stop new collection and cancel unsent automatic emails. Already submitted messages cannot be recalled. Contacts do not receive an automatic all-clear: tell them separately. If tracking time expires, your safety is still unconfirmed and manual contact options remain available.

3. What trusted contacts need to know

An alert can include an available map location or a private tracking link. Open the link in a browser: you do not need to install PPB or buy a subscription to view it. Up to three family viewers can follow simultaneously.

  • Check the recorded time, accuracy and status. A delayed or old point is not a fresh live location.
  • The viewer normally refreshes about every ten seconds. It may show live, delayed, offline, replaying or ended information.
  • Keep the link private. Someone with a forwarded link may be able to open it.
  • Contact the person and the appropriate local emergency service if concerned. Opening a viewer link does not dispatch help.

After an alert ends, new collection stops. Existing viewer access can remain for up to two hours, within the original session expiry. Remote session access expires no later than seven days after creation; this is not seven days of live tracking.

4. Choose your tracking plan

The free download includes one 15-minute tracking demo per verified PPB account, not a recurring daily or monthly allowance. Starting another alert or reinstalling under the same account does not renew it. Continued live tracking requires verified subscription access.

5. Quick troubleshooting

Email did not arrive

Check the connected Gmail sender, the selected contact's email, network access and the app's sending status. Queued is not sent, and accepted is not an inbox receipt. Gmail quotas and Android background delays can affect sending. Use another contact method when the result is failed or uncertain.

The map is old or no location appears

Check the point's timestamp, location consent, Android permissions, remaining tracking time, account connection and Emergency reliability checks. A fresh GPS fix is not always available. Without connectivity, locations may stay in the phone's bounded encrypted queue until reconnection. Historical replay does not extend live tracking time.

New phone setup is temporarily full

Your app account may be verified while the phone is not yet connected for remote tracking. Contact support or try again when setup capacity reopens. Use another available contact method in the meantime; a saved server address is not proof of a connection.

I bought a plan but access has not updated

Check that Google Play is using the account that made the purchase and that the phone is online. Refresh or restore access using the app's purchase controls. Google Play checkout and PPB's server verification are separate steps. Contact support before repeating a purchase unnecessarily.

What happens if the battery dies or the app is force-stopped?

PPB cannot guarantee operation after shutdown, force-stop, permission loss or a dead battery. Review Emergency reliability in More and the optional Android battery settings. No background setting guarantees uninterrupted service.

6. Your account and information

Your PPB login and optional Gmail connection are separate. Signing out does not renew the demo, disconnect Gmail or cancel a subscription. Disconnect Gmail from More > Automatic Gmail alerts to cancel unsent mail; submitted messages cannot be recalled.

To delete your app account and associated data, end active tracking and open More > App account > Delete app account & data. Confirm with your PPB password and complete any interrupted cleanup. You can also use the web deletion request. Messages already held by recipients are not erased by that process.

See the privacy policy for data handling and retention. For support, email davilapps@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, consent codes, private viewer links or precise location histories.
