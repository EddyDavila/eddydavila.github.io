GrowthAgent

Terms of Service

GrowthAgent is a Bitter Softworks desktop tool that prepares captions and illustrations for social media. It is in development and available for internal use and authorized testing. These terms apply when you use the tool.

Use and review

Use accounts and materials you are authorized to use. Review each caption and image before sending it to a platform. Check facts, rights, and any required advertising or AI disclosures. Generated illustrations can contain inaccuracies and should be identified as illustrations where needed.

TikTok sandbox

The optional TikTok integration is being prepared for sandbox testing. When enabled, it will send a selected photo and caption to an authorized TikTok account only after you approve the upload. You can review and edit the content from TikTok’s inbox notification and decide whether to post it in TikTok. TikTok’s upload guide

Your use of a connected platform is also subject to that platform’s terms and content rules.

Availability and support

Test features may change or be withdrawn. Delivery depends on the connected platform and may fail or be delayed. For questions about GrowthAgent, contact Bitter Softworks.
