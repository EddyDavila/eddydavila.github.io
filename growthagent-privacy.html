Privacy Policy

Bitter Softworks develops and operates GrowthAgent. This policy describes how the desktop tool handles information during internal use and authorized testing, including the planned TikTok sandbox integration.

Content and local records

GrowthAgent reads business listing details, such as the name, city, category, and subtype, to prepare captions and illustrations. It saves generated content and delivery records on the operator’s Windows computer. Connected publishing platforms receive the selected content. Where enabled, GrowthAgent saves aggregate Facebook post metrics locally to evaluate content performance.

Planned TikTok connection

TikTok handles sign-in and consent. The connector is designed to use the account identifier, display name, and username to verify the destination. It requests upload access to send a selected photo and caption. After you approve the upload, temporary media hosting would let TikTok retrieve the photo. TikTok would receive the caption and make the content available for review through its inbox. GrowthAgent would record the delivery identifier and status to verify the handoff and avoid duplicate attempts.

Storage and retention

GrowthAgent keeps local creative and delivery records for review, recovery, and troubleshooting until the operator removes them. Existing social credentials use encrypted Windows storage. The TikTok connector is designed to protect its authorization tokens in the same way. Staged media links will expire. The hosting provider may retain copies according to its storage settings. TikTok handles content it receives under its own privacy policy.

Access, revocation, and deletion

You can revoke GrowthAgent’s TikTok access through your TikTok account settings after connecting. For access, correction, deletion, or help removing local credentials and records, contact Bitter Softworks. Manage any content already in TikTok through TikTok.
